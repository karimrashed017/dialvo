# Feature parity: what the benchmark covers, and what Dialvo's site says

Written 19 Nov 2026 from a read of the benchmark's public site (home, inbound,
outbound, expansion, platform, 14 play pages and 13 platform feature pages) and
the founder's screenshots of its channel section. Everything here is in our own
words. We match **capabilities and the kind of sections**; we never reuse their
sentences, headlines, layout, colours, illustrations, agent names, customer
names or numbers, and the site never names them.

## 1. The positioning

A team of AI sales agents, not a single tool and not a cold caller:

- **Every channel:** voice, email, chat, social, SMS and WhatsApp, plus a dialer
  for human reps.
- **Three motions:** inbound (capture demand), outbound (create demand),
  expansion (grow existing customers).
- **One shared context:** every agent works from the CRM, a knowledge base,
  website activity and past conversations, plus live outside signals.
- **Reps keep the deals:** agents do the first contact, research, qualification
  and booking; people close.

Important detail we keep: **AI voice is for inbound leads and existing
customers who opted in.** Cold outbound is email and social; cold calls are
made by human reps using the dialer, with the research on screen.

## 3. Inbound

- Reply in under a minute, 24/7: chat on the site, or a voice callback after a
  form is filled in. A phone number or voice widget for the site.
- Qualification in the conversation: fit, need, timeline, budget, buying
  group; your own questions, conditional follow-ups and skip logic.
- Lead scoring against your ideal customer, using enrichment and history.
- Routing by territory, round-robin, named account or seniority; works with an
  existing router.
- Books on the right rep's calendar, or transfers the call live with a short
  briefing for the rep. Rescheduling and reminders.
- No answer: timed follow-ups by phone, SMS, email and WhatsApp.
- Not a fit yet: nurtured, redirected or flagged, never dropped.
- After-hours and multi-language intake.
- Full audit trail: recording, transcript, which questions were asked, why the
  outcome was tagged.
- Outcomes and notes written back to the CRM.

## 4. Outbound

- **Data:** a live company and contact database (firmographics, tech stack,
  org chart, funding, verified contact details), or your own CRM lists and
  CSVs. Records checked at the moment of outreach.
- **Signals:** funding, hiring, job changes, technology changes, website
  visits, review-site intent. Custom signals described in plain language
  ("companies that announced X in the last six months"), each match with its
  reasoning and sources.
- **Website visitor identification:** resolves visits to people and companies;
  the page they read shapes the outreach.
- **Lead qualification before outreach:** every lead checked against your
  criteria first.
- **Deep research:** a cited brief per contact from news, filings, job posts,
  the CRM and the knowledge base, with a suggested angle. You can steer it
  with directions.
- **AI personalisation:** one message per person, different by role, not a
  template with merge fields.
- **Multi-channel sequences:** email, social and rep-led dials in one sequence
  that carries context from touch to touch, with automatic fallback when a
  channel doesn't land.
- **Sequence builder:** steps, timing, channels, branching on opens, clicks
  and replies, send limits.
- **Smart replies:** sorts replies, answers questions and common objections
  from your positioning, books meetings, escalates the sensitive ones.
- **Campaign planner and preview:** turns goals and ideal customer into a
  plan; shows real research and messages for sample leads before anything
  sends.
- **Playbooks:** ready-made plays, each with its own audience and research
  logic; several can run at once; results per play.
- **Power dialer for reps:** who to call, why now, what to say.
- **Automatic opt-outs:** unsubscribes honoured at once and written to the CRM.

## 5. Expansion

- Triggers from CRM data, usage, renewal dates and account changes.
- Voice agents call existing customers who opted in; email, SMS and WhatsApp
  keep the conversation going.
- Upsell and cross-sell: more seats, a higher plan, a second product. Simple
  upgrades done on the call; bigger ones booked with a rep, context attached.
- Renewal check-ins, quiet-customer reactivation, free-to-paid conversion.
- Customer onboarding: answers new customers' calls and checks in, with
  permission, until they reach first value.
- Covers the whole customer base, not just the top accounts.

win-back · website visitor follow-up · event registration, follow-up and

## 10. Where we differ on purpose

- **No customer quotes, logos or result numbers.** Dialvo is in early access
  and has none to show. Sample screens use made-up businesses and are labelled
  "Example".
- **No certifications claimed** (they list security attestations; we list
  none).
- **No "by team" section and no managed-service tier.** Dialvo is self-serve
  for founders, agencies and small teams.
- **Public pricing, monthly, cancel anytime.** Theirs is sales-led.
- **No live "talk to the voice agent" widget yet.** The voice tab is a visual
  example; the sample call player sits under "You stay in control".
- **Review-site intent integration** is left off the site until we have it.
- **Language count:** we say "multilingual" without a number.
